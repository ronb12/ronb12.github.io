# Privacy Policy — Adelynn's Puppy Daycare

**Effective date:** July 26, 2026  
**Publisher:** Bradley Virtual Solutions, LLC  
**App:** Adelynn's Puppy Daycare (`com.puppydaycare.beta`)  
**Live page:** https://ronb12.github.io/puppy-daycare/privacy/

## Summary

Adelynn's Puppy Daycare is a family-friendly puppy daycare game for kids and parents. We design the app with **minimal data collection**, **no required account**, and **no App Tracking Transparency (ATT)** request for cross-app tracking.

## Information we collect

### On your device
- **Game progress** (puppies, coins, unlocks, missions, settings) is saved locally on your device using Apple’s standard on-device storage (UserDefaults / app documents).
- Progress stays on the device unless you choose optional Game Center features (below).

### Game Center (optional)
- Signing in to **Game Center** is optional. The full game works without it.
- If you sign in, Apple may process leaderboard scores and achievement progress under [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- We do **not** operate a separate username/password account system.

### Advertising (iOS only)
- The **iOS** app may show Google **AdMob** banner ads and optional rewarded ads.
- Ads are configured with **child / age-restricted treatment** and a **General (G)** max ad content rating where supported.
- We do **not** request ATT permission for cross-app tracking (`NSPrivacyTracking` = false).
- Google may process device advertising identifiers and related signals according to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can limit ad personalization in device settings and via Google’s tools.
- **Mac builds do not load ads.**

### What we do not collect
- We do not require creating an account with us.
- We do not knowingly sell personal information.
- We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health, or payment card data in the core game loop.
- We do not use our own analytics SDK beyond what Apple or AdMob may provide as part of their platforms.
- There are **no in-app purchases** or subscription paywalls in the current version.

## Children’s privacy

This game is intended for a general audience including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own profiles or marketing lists.

Parents / caregivers can:
- Use Screen Time and content restrictions on the device
- Sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings
- Limit personalized ads in device privacy settings

For AdMob’s child-directed / under-age treatment, see [Google’s documentation for families](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431).

## Data sharing

We share data only as needed to operate the app:

| Partner | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Apple** | App Store distribution, device OS services, optional Game Center |
| **Google (AdMob)** | Serving ads on iOS only |

We do not sell personal information.

## Your choices

- Reset local save from **Settings** in the app (confirmation required).
- Decline or ignore Game Center sign-in — gameplay continues.
- Limit ad tracking / personalized ads in device settings.
- Contact us (below) with privacy questions or deletion requests for any data you believe we hold (local saves are under your device control).
