Summary

Adelynn's Puppy Daycare is a family-friendly puppy daycare game for kids and parents. We design the app with minimal data collection, no required account, and no App Tracking Transparency (ATT) request for cross-app tracking.

Information we collect

On your device

  • Game progress (puppies, coins, unlocks, missions, settings) is saved locally using Apple’s on-device storage (UserDefaults / app documents).
  • Progress stays on the device unless you choose optional Game Center features.

Game Center (optional)

  • Signing in to Game Center is optional. The full game works without it.
  • If you sign in, Apple may process leaderboard scores and achievement progress under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • We do not operate a separate username/password account system.

Advertising (iOS only)

  • The iOS app may show Google AdMob banner ads and optional rewarded ads.
  • Ads use child / age-restricted treatment and a General (G) max ad content rating where supported.
  • We do not request ATT for cross-app tracking (NSPrivacyTracking = false).
  • Google may process device advertising identifiers and related signals per Google’s Privacy Policy. Limit personalization in device settings and via Google’s tools.
  • Mac builds do not load ads.

What we do not collect

  • No account with us is required.
  • We do not knowingly sell personal information.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health, or payment card data in the core game loop.
  • We do not use our own analytics SDK beyond what Apple or AdMob may provide as part of their platforms.
  • There are no in-app purchases or subscription paywalls in the current version.

Children’s privacy

This game is intended for a general audience including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own profiles or marketing lists.

Parents / caregivers can:

  • Use Screen Time and content restrictions on the device
  • Sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings
  • Limit personalized ads in device privacy settings

For AdMob’s child-directed / under-age treatment, see Google’s documentation for families.
