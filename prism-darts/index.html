Prism Darts

A carnival balloon booth for iPhone. Drag from the dart stand, throw, and pop colorful balloons. Matching colors builds a combo.

How to play

  • Drag from the wooden stand and release to throw
  • One dart is in the air at a time, and you have 3 lives
  • Match candy colors to raise your combo
  • Gold, steel, bomb, heart, prism, and camouflage balloons change the round
  • Storm balloons cost a life if they float off the top
  • Optional Game Center adds ranks, achievements, and friend duels

Privacy

  • No ads, analytics, or In-App Purchases
  • Progress stays on device unless you use optional Game Center
