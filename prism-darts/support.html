How to play

  • Tap Play, Daily, or Duel.
  • Drag from the wooden stand toward a balloon, then release to throw.
  • One dart is in the air at a time (or a 3-dart spread during Triple Shot). You have 3 lives.
  • Matching candy colors builds a combo. Gold balloons pay extra without breaking the streak.
  • Steel takes two hits. Bombs pop nearby balloons. Hearts restore a life. Prism grants Triple Shot. Camouflage balloons are faint.
  • A storm balloon that floats off the top takes a life.
  • Earn tickets by playing and spend them on dart skins and booth themes. There are no In-App Purchases.

Game Center

Leaderboards, achievements, cloud save, and friend duels are optional and use Apple Game Center. You can play the booth without signing in.

Data

Progress stays on your iPhone unless you sign in to Game Center. There are no ads or In-App Purchases. See the Privacy Policy.
