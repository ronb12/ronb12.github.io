Prism Darts Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026
Developer: Bradley Virtual Solutions, LLC

Prism Darts is a carnival balloon dart game for iPhone. You can play the booth without creating an account with us. We do not run ads, analytics SDKs, or In-App Purchases.

Data we collect

Bradley Virtual Solutions does not independently collect, sell, or share personal data.

Score, tickets, equipped cosmetics, and daily-challenge reminders stay on your device using Apple’s on-device storage. Deleting the app removes them.

Optional Apple services

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple may store your Game Center player identity, leaderboard scores, achievements, friend duels, and an optional cloud save of your progress. That data is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not operate our own game servers.

Prism Darts may ask for permission to send a local notification when a new Daily Challenge is available. That reminder is scheduled on your device and is not sent to us.

Children

Prism Darts is a casual arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no account registration with us and no developer-operated online profile.
